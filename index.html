Applied AI · Raybaby

AI-powered IoT sleep & breathing monitoring

Raybaby is a non-contact baby monitor that uses AI to track sleep and breathing. As backend lead I built the data side that fed and served its models:

  • Big data handling: continuous sensor streams from devices over MQTT, processed with Celery and Redis on AWS.
  • Training data: cleaning, shaping and preparing device data with DataFrames for model training.
  • AI predictions: integrated the prediction algorithms with the DSP engineers and served the results through Django REST APIs.
  • Production at scale: load balancing, monitoring with Sentry, and over-the-air firmware updates.
